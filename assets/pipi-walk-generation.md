## 八方向补充视角：n

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent 3D Pipi mascot walking sprite key-pose atlas.
Reference roles: image 1 defines exact Pipi identity and physical proportions, image 2 defines the walking rendering/material and folded wings, image 3 provides natural stepping anatomy.
Create exactly EIGHT isolated full-body parrots in a strict 4-column by 2-row grid, read left to right then top to bottom. Genuine alpha transparency, no text, numbers, shadows, floor, cards or motion marks. Large clear margins around every feather and toe.
VIEW LOCK: directly REAR-facing, walking AWAY from the viewer toward the TOP of the screen. Show the smooth green BACK of the round head, rear crown feathers, both folded wings and layered blue/green tail. NO eyes, NO face patches and NO beak visible from this rear view.
All eight poses must keep this identical direction, camera angle, head shape/size, torso size and lighting. Big green round head, yellow-green crest, cream face only when visible from this direction, orange feet, green/yellow/blue folded wings, blue-green tail. Soft polished toy-like 3D mascot shading. Same gentle smile if the beak is visible; no O mouth.
This is a WALK with ALTERNATING legs, never a hop, kick-loop or flight. At least one foot touches the ground in EVERY pose. Keep one shared ground plane and head height, modest hip movement. No wings spreading.
Eight chronological phases:
1. First foot reaches forward along the stated travel direction, heel contacts ground; second foot is behind, toe planted.
2. First foot bears weight flat on the ground, second foot begins lifting from behind.
3. First foot is planted under the body; second foot passes it, lifted just clear of the ground with bent ankle.
4. First foot trails behind with toe on ground; second foot reaches forward, just above the ground.
5. SECOND foot now contacts forward; FIRST foot is behind on toe. This swaps the leading leg from pose 1.
6. Second foot bears weight on ground; first foot lifts behind.
7. Second foot planted under body; first foot passes it in the air.
8. Second foot trails on ground; first foot reaches ahead just before contacting, leading seamlessly back to pose 1.
Keep feet recognizable as rounded cartoon parrot toes, naturally changing sole/ankle angle through the step. Legs connect to the belly. The body and head remain steady in scale and orientation. Single clean outlines, complete body, no clipping or motion blur.
```

4 UP A: NEAR foot trails behind on toe; FAR foot has swung ahead toward LEFT, toes/heel just above floor, ready to contact.
5 CONTACT B: FAR foot extended ahead to LEFT, heel contacts floor; NEAR foot trails to RIGHT with toes on floor. The opposite leg leads compared with pose 1.
6 DOWN B: Weight settles onto FAR planted foot; NEAR foot lifts behind.
7 PASS B: FAR foot planted under hip; NEAR foot lifted and passing forward under belly.
8 UP B: FAR foot trails on toe; NEAR foot reaches ahead toward LEFT just above floor, ready to return seamlessly to pose 1.
Natural small bird strut: clear alternating steps, soft articulation, gentle weight transfer. Keep both wings folded. Smooth toy-like 3D shading, clean single feather edges, no motion blur or ghost outlines. Exact same facial expression and head pose throughout.

## 成品处理

五组视角（正面、左侧前、左侧、左侧后、背面）来自内置 image_gen；右侧三个对应视角逐帧镜像。提取完整生成脚掌，在固定头部和躯干的基础上制作交替支撑、抬脚和落脚，保留 3.2px 的周期性重心起伏。较小的绿色踝部区域随脚掌连接移动；不拉伸脚趾。起步与停步经过相邻视角，首尾均为原始默认姿态。早期六帧转身候选保留作对照，最终转身使用八方向的统一站姿。

每方向成品 61 帧，单格 480×576，长图 29280×576。0–13 起步，14–45 循环步态，46–60 停步；以上索引从 0 开始。逐帧时长、步幅、支撑脚和默认图定位保存在同名 JSON 中。
